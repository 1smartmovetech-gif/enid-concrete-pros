# Buy Box

This is the decision filter for incoming deals.

## Core Questions

- What strategy is this for?
  - Buy and hold
  - Flip
  - Owner finance
  - Wholesale
  - Seller finance acquisition

- What market is acceptable?
- What price range is acceptable?
- What rehab level is acceptable?
- What minimum spread / equity do we need?
- What minimum cash flow do we need?
- What seller situations are preferred?

## Pass Rules

Auto-pass if:
- title or legal issue is too messy for current bandwidth
- rehab scope exceeds available capital / management capacity
- area is outside approved market unless exception is justified
- no clear exit strategy

## Scoring

Score each lead 1-5 on:
- Motivation
- Margin
- Market
- Ease of execution
- Financing fit

Total score:
- 20-25 = high priority
- 15-19 = maybe / needs deeper review
- below 15 = likely pass
