## Screening Questions for Potential Attorneys

### Initial Consultation Questions:
1. "Do you have experience with installment land contracts in Indiana?"
2. "Have you worked with private money lenders on real estate transactions?"
3. "Are you familiar with Vanderburgh County recording practices?"
4. "What is your experience with Indiana's land contract statutes (IC 32-28-3)?"
5. "Do you represent real estate investors regularly?"
6. "What is your fee structure for document review and drafting?"
7. "Can you assist with recording documents remotely?"
8. "Do you have experience with virtual closings and electronic signatures in Indiana?"

### Specific Issues to Discuss:
1. Contract math error correction
2. Proper recording of mortgage and memorandum
3. Insurance coordination between parties
4. Tax payment verification systems
5. Default remedy options under Indiana law
6. Required Indiana disclosures
7. Electronic signature validity
8. Title insurance coordination

## Cost Considerations

### Typical Fee Structures:
1. **Hourly Rates:** $250-$450/hour for experienced attorneys
2. **Flat Fees:** $500-$2,000 for document package review
3. **Package Deals:** May offer investor packages
4. **Recording Fees:** Additional $100-$300 for recording services

### Budget Recommendations:
- Initial Consultation: $200-$400
- Document Review: $500-$1,500
- Document Drafting: $750-$2,000
- Recording Assistance: $200-$500
- Ongoing Questions: Hourly or retainer

## Preparation for Attorney Consultation

### Documents to Provide:
1. Both PDF contracts (loan agreement and land contract)
2. Title commitment/insurance policy
3. Property information sheet
4. Party contact information
5. Any correspondence with buyer/lender

### Questions to Prepare:
1. How to correct the $179,000 vs. $77,900 discrepancy?
2. Best recording strategy for both documents?
3. Insurance requirements for all parties?
4. Tax payment verification method?
5. Default management procedures?
