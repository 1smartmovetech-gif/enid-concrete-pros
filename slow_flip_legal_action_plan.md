# Slow Flip Legal Action Plan

## Executive Summary
You have a slow flip transaction with two key documents that need immediate legal review and correction. The Private Money Loan Agreement and Contract for Deed contain critical issues that could expose you, your lender, and your buyer to significant risk.

## Critical Issues Identified

### 1. Contract Mathematical Error
- **Purchase Price:** $79,900
- **Down Payment:** $2,000  
- **Amount Financed:** Listed as $179,000 (should be $77,900)
- **Impact:** This error could invalidate the contract or create confusion about the actual debt amount.

### 2. Missing Essential Documents
- **Promissory Note** referenced but not provided
- **Recorded Mortgage/Deed of Trust** status unknown
- **Lender's Title Insurance** not confirmed
- **Proper Insurance Documentation** missing

### 3. Indiana Legal Compliance Concerns
- Land contract default remedies (forfeiture vs. foreclosure)
- Required Indiana disclosures missing
- Electronic signature validity under Indiana law
- Late fee compliance with Indiana usury laws

## Immediate Action Items (Next 7 Days)

### 1. Document Correction
- **Fix mathematical error** in Contract for Deed
- **Create missing Promissory Note** for lender
- **Verify recording status** of security instruments

### 2. Attorney Engagement
- **Contact 3-5 Indiana real estate attorneys** (see list below)
- **Schedule consultations** to discuss specific issues
- **Retain attorney** with land contract experience

### 3. Risk Mitigation
- **Freeze further payments** until documents corrected
- **Notify parties** of document review process
- **Secure insurance verification** from buyer
