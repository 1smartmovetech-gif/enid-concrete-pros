# Site Build Spec

## Reference model
The reference site pattern is a classic local service SEO site:
- strong local H1
- call-driven hero
- service cards
- supporting service pages
- locations page
- simple about/contact pages
- FAQ and trust-building content

## Important rule
We are copying the **structure and conversion model**, not the competitor's copy, claims, brand, images, or business identity.

## Build standard
- one niche
- one city
- one primary keyword
- five supporting services
- independent local guide positioning by default
- placeholder phone until a real tracking number exists
- no fake reviews, no fake office, no fake staff, no fake licenses

## Recommended page set
- Home
- About This Guide
- Contact / Request Quotes
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Use
- Editorial Policy
- Service Area
- Main service page
- 5 supporting service pages
- Cost guide
- How to choose a provider
- FAQs

## Homepage pattern
1. local headline
2. brief explanation of what the site helps users do
3. primary services grid
4. why compare providers
5. service area summary
6. request quote CTA
7. educational links
8. FAQs
9. independent-guide disclaimer

## Service page pattern
1. H1 with service + location
2. service need/problem intro
3. common service options
4. pricing factors
5. questions to ask
6. warning signs
7. permits / insurance / licensing considerations where relevant
8. local Oklahoma considerations
9. CTA form
10. FAQs
11. internal links
