## Specific Legal Concerns

### 1. Indiana Land Contract Law
- Indiana Code § 32-28-3 governs land contracts
- Courts may treat long-term contracts as mortgages
- Forfeiture remedies have specific notice requirements
- 30-day cure period for defaults is standard

### 2. Usury Law
- Indiana usury limit: 25% for loans over $50,000
- Both loans appear within limits (12% and 13%)
- Late fees may be scrutinized if excessive

### 3. Title Issues
- Need to verify clean title from seller to Rural Haven Properties
- Need to verify first lien position for lender
- Need to address any existing liens or encumbrances

### 4. Insurance Coordination
- Lender needs to be loss payee on insurance
- Seller needs to be additional insured
- These must be coordinated, not conflicting
