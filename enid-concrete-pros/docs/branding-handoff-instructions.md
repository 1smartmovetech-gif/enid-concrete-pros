# Instructions for Changing Branding, Phone, Email, and Form Destination

## Branding
- replace the site title with the final brand name
- upload the logo and replace placeholder logo block
- update favicon

## Phone
- replace every `[PHONE NUMBER]` instance
- update click-to-call links
- update tracking number in header, footer, and CTA blocks

## Email
- replace `[EMAIL ADDRESS]` on contact and policy pages

## Form destination
- connect the form to GoHighLevel, email, webhook, or WP form notifications
- test successful submission and notification delivery

## Ownership disclaimer
- if sold to an operator, update the disclaimer language to reflect the new ownership and service relationship
