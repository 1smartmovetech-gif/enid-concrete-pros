# GHL Form Text Replacement

Use this text inside the GoHighLevel form instead of the template placeholders.

## Non-marketing SMS consent
By checking this box, I consent to receive non-marketing text messages related to my concrete service request, quote follow-up, scheduling, and project communication. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

## Marketing SMS consent
By checking this box, I consent to receive occasional marketing and promotional text messages about concrete services, offers, and updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

## If you want the business name referenced
Use:
- `Enid Concrete Pros`

Then the first line becomes:
By checking this box, I consent to receive non-marketing text messages from Enid Concrete Pros related to my concrete service request, quote follow-up, scheduling, and project communication.

And the second line becomes:
By checking this box, I consent to receive occasional marketing and promotional text messages from Enid Concrete Pros about concrete services, offers, and updates.

## Recommendation
If you do not actually plan to send marketing texts yet, disable the marketing checkbox entirely and keep only the service/request communication consent.
