Common repair issues

  • Surface cracks
  • Spalling or flaking
  • Edge wear

What to ask before repairing

  • Is the issue cosmetic or structural?
  • Is water part of the problem?
  • Will repair be enough, or should replacement be compared?
  • How visible will the repair remain?

Repair versus replacement

Some concrete problems stay localized and can be repaired. Others are symptoms of movement, drainage trouble, or broader slab failure. Comparing both paths can help avoid spending money on a repair that will not hold up.

Cosmetic damage is not the only question

Small cracks may look minor, but water intrusion, scaling, and uneven movement can make the issue bigger over time. A useful quote should help clarify whether the problem is surface-level or part of a larger failure pattern.
