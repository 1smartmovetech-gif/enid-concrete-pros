Planning around drainage and layout

Patios need more than a good finish. Slope, water runoff, nearby structures, and how the surface connects to doors or walkways all affect whether the space stays useful and easy to maintain over time.

Choosing the right finish

Some homeowners want a simple broom finish while others want more decorative detail. It helps to compare finish style, maintenance expectations, and whether the quote includes edge or border work.
