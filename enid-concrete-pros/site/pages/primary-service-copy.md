## Enid and Oklahoma considerations
Concrete in Oklahoma takes more abuse than many homeowners expect. High summer heat, winter cold spells, shifting moisture levels, and subgrade movement can all affect cracking, settlement, and surface wear. A contractor who understands drainage, expansion joints, and proper prep may help reduce problems later.

## Cost factors
Price can vary based on total square footage, access to the site, removal of old concrete, sub-base work, finish complexity, reinforcement, and whether grading or drainage corrections are needed before the pour.

## Questions to ask providers
- How will the base be prepared?
- How will drainage be handled?
- What joint plan is being used?
- What parts of the quote cover removal, grading, and cleanup?
- Are there conditions that could change the scope after demolition?
