# Frequently Asked Questions

## What causes concrete to crack?
Concrete can crack for many reasons, including shrinkage, drainage problems, soil movement, freeze-thaw stress, and normal expansion and contraction.

## Is every crack a sign of major failure?
No. Some cracks are cosmetic, while others point to settlement, water problems, or poor preparation. The pattern, width, and movement matter.

## When does repair make sense?
Repair may make sense when the issue is limited, the surface is still structurally usable, and the problem source can be addressed.

## When is replacement worth comparing?
Replacement is often worth comparing when there is widespread cracking, poor drainage, significant settlement, broken edges, or repeated repair history.

## Does Oklahoma weather affect concrete differently?
Yes. Heat, winter cold snaps, moisture changes, and local soil behavior can all affect cracking and long-term movement.

## What affects the price of a concrete project?
Project size, demolition, finish complexity, access, grading, reinforcement, drainage corrections, and local labor conditions can all affect price.

## What should I ask before hiring a contractor?
Ask about base prep, slope and drainage, joint placement, reinforcement, timeline, cleanup, and what could change the quote after demolition or excavation.
