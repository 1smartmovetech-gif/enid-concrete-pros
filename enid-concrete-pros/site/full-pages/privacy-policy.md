# Privacy Policy

Enid Concrete Pros is an independent local guide. If you submit information through a form on this website, that information may be used to review your request and route it to a local provider or system designated to respond.

## Information that may be collected
- name
- phone number
- email address
- ZIP code
- requested service type
- project description
- preferred contact method

## How information may be used
- to review quote requests
- to contact you about your request
- to route your inquiry to a local provider or lead-handling system
- to improve the site and its forms

## Important note
Submitting a request does not create a contractor-client relationship with this website.
