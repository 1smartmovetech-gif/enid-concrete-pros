# Editorial Policy

Enid Concrete Pros is designed to publish useful local service information without fake claims or misleading business details.

## Editorial standards
- use plain, readable language
- avoid fabricated reviews, project counts, staff bios, or licensing claims
- distinguish general estimate guidance from verified facts
- keep local pages specific to Enid and Oklahoma conditions
- avoid thin city-name-swapped content

## Review focus
Pages should be reviewed for:
- clarity
- duplicate content risk
- placeholder cleanup
- factual accuracy within the limits of a local guide site
- conversion readiness without misleading claims

## Corrections and updates
When ownership, phone numbers, or operational details change, the site content and disclaimers should be updated accordingly.
