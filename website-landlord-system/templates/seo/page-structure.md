# Local SEO Page Structure

## Homepage
- H1: service + city
- opening benefit statement
- call CTA
- short form
- service bullets
- why choose us
- process section
- FAQ
- service area section
- final CTA

## Supporting pages
- service subtype page 1
- service subtype page 2
- service subtype page 3
- pricing / cost guide
- FAQ page if needed

## Example cluster for junk removal
- junk removal Austin
- furniture removal Austin
- appliance removal Austin
- garage cleanout Austin
- estate cleanout Austin
- junk removal cost Austin

## Copy rules
- include the city naturally
- include intent phrases, not stuffing
- add unique local details
- keep sentences simple
- write for conversion first, SEO second
