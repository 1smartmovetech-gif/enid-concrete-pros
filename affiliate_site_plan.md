# Affiliate Site Plan

## Goal
Build a niche affiliate website for prop firm traders that highlights discounts, comparisons, reviews, and recommended tools/services.

## Reference Site Reviewed
- https://www.prolongwealth.com/
- Public branding appears to center on PropFirmSave.com / futures funding deals.

## What the reference site seems to be doing
Based on public pages/search results, the site appears to use this model:
- Niche focus on futures prop firm funding / trader audience
- Content + community credibility angle
- Affiliate/deal aggregation
- Likely simple CMS/site-builder structure rather than a heavy custom app
- Pages for home, about, blog, and deal/info pages

## What to copy
- Narrow niche focus
- Simple deal-oriented messaging
- Community/experience positioning
- Easy path from page visit to affiliate click
- Educational + comparison content around offers

## What to improve

- Better deal comparison tables
- Stronger SEO page structure
- Better disclaimers and FTC affiliate disclosures
- More obvious "best for" segmentation
- Email capture / lead magnet

## Google Sites assessment
### Can Google Sites work?
Yes — for a fast MVP.

### Pros
- Fast to launch
- No coding required
- Easy editing
- Free/low friction
- Good enough for basic pages and links

### Cons
- Limited SEO control compared with WordPress/Webflow/custom sites
- Limited design flexibility
- Weak comparison-table and affiliate-SEO features
- Harder to scale into a serious content site
- Less control over structured content and templates

### Recommendation
- Use Google Sites only if the goal is to launch a simple MVP quickly.
- If the goal is long-term SEO traffic and serious affiliate growth, WordPress is the stronger option.

## Best practical approach
### Phase 1: MVP
- Build on Google Sites if speed matters
- Create 5–8 core pages
- Add top affiliate offers manually
- Validate interest and messaging

### Phase 2: Growth
- Move to WordPress or another more SEO-friendly platform
- Add many review/comparison pages
- Improve internal linking and search optimization
- Add lead capture and possibly email automation
