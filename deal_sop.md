# Deal SOP

## Purpose
This SOP defines the order of operations for taking a lead from first sight through closing, resale, and servicing.

## Stage 1: Lead Capture
1. Create a deal folder.
2. Enter address, parcel, source, seller contact, and asking price.
3. Save listing screenshots or lead source evidence.
4. Tag likely deal type: wholesale, cash flip, slow flip, or unknown.

## Stage 2: Fast Screen
1. Check map, street view, listing photos, and neighborhood quality.
2. Estimate rough value, repair level, and likely exit.
3. Decide: pursue, low offer, follow up, or pass.
4. Log why.

## Stage 3: Preliminary Underwriting
1. Pull ownership and tax data.
2. Review quick comps.
3. Estimate total all-in basis.
4. Estimate exit price and buyer terms if slow flip.
5. Establish ideal offer and max safe offer.

## Stage 4: Seller Contact and Offer
1. Contact seller or source.
2. Confirm motivation, condition, occupancy, and timeline.
3. Make concise offer.
4. Document all communication.
5. Set follow-up date.

## Stage 5: Contracting
1. Use the correct acquisition agreement for the deal type.
2. Confirm contingencies and due diligence period.
3. Deposit earnest money if appropriate.
4. Open title / escrow / attorney file.
5. Save the executed contract in the deal folder.

## Stage 6: Deep Due Diligence
1. Order title work.
2. Confirm taxes, liens, and ownership.
3. Confirm insurance availability.
4. Confirm property condition through inspection, photos, agent, contractor, or trusted local source.
5. Re-run comps and exit plan.
6. Decide whether to proceed, renegotiate, or cancel.

## Stage 7: Funding Preparation
1. Confirm funding source.
2. Prepare loan package if using private money.
3. Verify wire instructions.
4. Verify closing costs and cash needed.
5. Finalize closing calendar.

## Stage 8: Close
1. Review settlement statement.
2. Execute closing documents.
3. Confirm deed and any security instruments are recorded.
4. Save signed and recorded documents.
5. Update deal tracker with final basis.

## Stage 9: Disposition Prep
1. Define exit route.
2. Prepare listing copy, photos, and terms.
3. Set asking price, down payment target, and monthly payment target.
4. Prepare buyer screening process.
5. Prepare required resale docs.

## Stage 10: Buyer Screening
1. Capture buyer identity.
2. Review income, stability, and seriousness.
3. Confirm they understand payment, maintenance, taxes, insurance, and default terms.
4. Collect verified funds for down payment.
5. Approve or reject buyer.

## Stage 11: Resale Closing
1. Execute the correct resale agreement and finance documents.
2. Deliver disclosures.
3. Confirm insurance requirements are satisfied.
4. Set up payment servicing.
5. Record any required instruments or memoranda.
6. Save complete resale file.

## Stage 12: Servicing
1. Send payment instructions.
2. Track monthly payments.
3. Track taxes and insurance.
4. Send late notices on time.
5. Log all communication.
6. Escalate defaults per state-specific process.

## Stage 13: Portfolio Review
1. Review cash flow monthly.
2. Review delinquencies.
3. Review taxes and insurance.
4. Review whether reserves are adequate.
5. Review whether any file needs legal action.

## Non-Negotiables
- No address without a folder.
- No close without clear title status.
- No slow flip without a defined servicing process.
- No state-specific owner-finance paperwork without local legal review.
- No deal that only works if everything goes perfectly.
